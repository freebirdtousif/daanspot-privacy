Privacy Policy – DaanSpot

“See a need. Be the help.”

Effective Date: October 23, 2026

DaanSpot (“we,” “our,” or “the App”) helps people discover nearby locations where assistance may be needed, share those locations, and record donations. We respect your privacy and aim to handle your information responsibly.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, DaanSpot may process the following information:

  • Location Information: With your permission, the App may access your device location to show nearby help spots and support location-related features. You can manage location permissions in your device settings. If you create a help spot, the location you submit may be visible to other users.
  • Authentication Information: The App may use Firebase Authentication, including anonymous authentication, to identify an app session and support app features. If you sign in using another method, the relevant authentication information may also be processed.
  • Help Spot Information: Information you submit when creating a spot, such as its location, category, description, and any optional information or images supported by the App, may be stored and displayed to other users.
  • Donation History: When you record a donation, the App may store the associated spot, date, and related activity information to display your donation history.
  • Technical Information: The App's hosting or service providers may process technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the App.

We do not intend to collect more information than is reasonably necessary for the App's features.

2. How We Use Information

  • Display help spots and nearby assistance opportunities.
  • Allow users to create and share help spots.
  • Record donations and display donation history.
  • Provide authentication and maintain app functionality.
  • Protect the App against misuse, fraud, and security threats.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve reliability.

3. Sharing and Public Information

Help spot details that you submit for discovery may be visible to other DaanSpot users. Do not include sensitive personal information in a spot description.

If you choose to share a spot using WhatsApp or another sharing service, information you choose to share will be handled according to that service's privacy policy.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate the App, or when required by applicable law.

4. Third-Party Services

DaanSpot may use third-party services such as Firebase for authentication, database, hosting, or other app functions, and OpenStreetMap-based services to display maps.

  • Firebase Privacy Information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • OpenStreetMap Privacy Information: https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's features, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service.

You may contact us to request access to or deletion of personal information associated with your use of DaanSpot. We may need to verify your request and retain certain information where legally required.

6. Children's Privacy

DaanSpot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices

You can manage device location permissions through your Android settings. You should also avoid including private or sensitive information in publicly visible help spots.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published at the same policy URL with a revised effective date.
